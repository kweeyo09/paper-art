# 一纸生花 — design contract
## Visual world
An intimate Chinese paper-craft table. Vermilion cut paper is the focal object, framed by warm ivory, fine ink rules, small seal marks and generous negative space. Chinese by default with a complete English toggle; decorative seals retain their Chinese calligraphy.
## Surface
Operate mode: left-hand fold, color and tools; large central cutting table; narrow strip of real generated pattern examples. On small screens examples flow underneath. No decorative marketing sections separate the user from the craft.
## Typography and color
Noto Serif SC for editorial headings; Ma Shan Zheng for tiny seal lettering. Readable system Chinese sans for operational text. Ivory #f5f1e8, paper #faf7ef, ink #332f28, vermilion #ae302a.
## Interaction
Main authored motion: folded sectors fan around their shared origin on reveal. Reduced motion skips animation. Guides are never exported. Every pattern thumbnail is generated from the same clipping geometry as the editor.
